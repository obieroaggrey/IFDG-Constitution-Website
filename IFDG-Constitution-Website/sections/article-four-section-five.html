ARTICLE FOUR

SECTION V

ANNUAL AUDIT

The Auditor

An auditor shall be appointed by members for the following year by the annual meeting. No auditor shall be an office bearer or member of the committee of the association.

All the Group's accounts records and documents shall be opened to the inspection of the auditor on request through the chairperson.

The Management Committee shall maintain Books of Accounts

The Books and Accounts of the Group shall be audited each year by the Auditors.

Copies of the Audited Accounts shall be sent to every member of the Group twenty-one (21) days before the meeting at which the Accounts are to be considered.

The Audited Accounts shall be discussed and approved at the General Meeting of members. The Financial year of the Association shall end on the 31st of December of each year.

The Auditors of the Association shall be the Auditors of the Ministry.
