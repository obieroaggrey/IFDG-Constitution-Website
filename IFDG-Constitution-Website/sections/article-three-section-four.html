ARTICLE THREE – SECTION IV

Qualifications for Leadership Positions

Chairperson

i. Must be able to read and write with a minimum of a certificate in tertiary level

ii. Must be a Kenyan citizen

iii. Must be above 18 years

iv. Must have atleast 10% of the groups available share

v. Must hqave been a member of the group for a period not less than 6 months

vi. Must have a history of integrity

vIce chairperson

i. Must be a Kenyan citizen

Must be above 18 years

ii. Must have at least 5% of the group’s available share.

iii. Must have been a member of the group for a period not less than six months

iv. Must have a history of integrity.

Secretary General

i. Must Must be able to read and write with a minimum of a certificate in tertiary level

ii. Must be a Kenyan citizen

Must be above 18 years

iii. Must have at least 5% of the group’s available share.

iv. Must have been a member of the group for a period not less than six months

Treasurer

i. Must be able to read and write and must have good knowledge in Microsoft excel.

ii. Have proper understanding of the basic accounting principles

iii. Must be a Kenyan citizen

Must be above 18 years

iv. Must have at least 5% of the group’s available share.

v. Must have been a member of the group for a period not less than eight months.

vi. Must have history of integrity and have no record of defaulting with the loans given to him/her by the group
