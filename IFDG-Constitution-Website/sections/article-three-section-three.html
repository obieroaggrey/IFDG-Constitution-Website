ARTICLE THREE – SECTION III

OFFICE BEARERS

The policy and management of the affairs of the Group shall be directed by a Management Committee which shall meet at least once a month and shall consist of not less than three members.

DUTIES OF THE MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE

❖ The Committee shall be responsible for the Management of the Group.

❖ The Committee shall have power to appoint such sub-committees, as it shall deem desirable to make reports to the Committee upon which such action shall be taken as seems to the committee desirable.

❖ All monies disbursed on behalf of the Group shall be authorized by the committee.

❖ The quorum for meetings of the management committee shall not be less than two thirds (2/3) of its members.

OFFICE BEARERS OF THE GROUP

The office bearers of the Group shall be:

i) A chairperson

ii) A vice chairperson

iii) A secretary general

iv) A treasurer

v) A project coordinator

All office bearers shall be fully members of the Group.

❖ All office bearers shall be elected at Annual General Meeting (AGM) to be held each year and shall hold the office till the succeeding AGM and shall be eligible for reelection for a second term.

❖ Any office bearer who ceases to be a member of the Group shall automatically cease to be an office bearer thereof.

❖ Office bearers may be removed from office as a result of gross misconduct as shall be determined by a disciplinary committee which shall be constituted at the time the secretary general in the event of the chair and deputy being incapacitated.

❖ The highest ranking member of the Group shall call for an emergency meeting where members shall on a temporary basis appoint any member to fill such vacancy in event of such occurrence.

DUTIES OF OFFICE BEARERS

The Chairperson

❖ Shall convene and preside over all meetings of the Association. In his absence, the General Secretary shall assume this duty.

❖ Shall be a signatory to the accounts of the Association, and sign all documents on behalf of the Association.

❖ Shall be the chief spokesman of the Association at all meetings.

❖ Shall see to the implementation of the Management Committee’s decisions, and shall perform any other business as may be directed by the Management Committee.

❖ Shall submit a report about the plans, decisions, and activities of the Group to the members annually.

❖ Shall, in consultation with the Management Committee nominate members of various committees, subject to the approval of the members.

❖ Shall be the custodian of all the Group’s properties.

Vice Chairperson

❖ Apart from deputizing the chair he shall be in charge of all the group’s investments and all the projects of the group.

❖ He shall represent the interest of the minority in the management committee.

❖ Shall address all matters that require the attention of the chairperson in case the chairperson is unreachable.

Secretary General

❖ With the approval of the chairman, preside over meetings, issue notices and agenda or details of the business to be transacted at all general meetings not less than 48 hours before the scheduled meetings.

❖ Keep and maintain true and accurate minutes and records of all proceedings of the general assembly and council meetings.

❖ In consultation with the chairman handle all correspondence of the Association.

❖ Compile and maintain an official list of all members of the association.

❖ Perform any other duties as may be assigned to him/her by the chairman.

❖ Be a signatory to the account of the Association.

Treasurer/Financial Secretary shall

❖ Keep and maintain proper books of accounts of the Fund and any other income accruing to the Association.

❖ Prepare financial statements for presentation to the general meeting and for audit purposes at the end of each term and at any other time upon request by two-thirds (2/3) majority of the general meeting within seven (7) days’ notice.

❖ Lodge all monies belonging to the Association with a designated bank and obtain receipts for all such transactions.

❖ Have custody of all the Association’s checkbooks and cash books.

❖ Be a signatory to the account of the Group.

Project coordinator

❖ organizing and planning of all events of the group including schedule of meetings and all conventions held by or in the name of the group with reference to the chair.

❖ budgeting and planning for the group’s events such as the AGM

TENURE OF OFFICE

The Management Committee shall serve a term of one (1) year. The one-year term begins on 12th January and ends on 12th January of the following year.

Where a member is found not to be performing effectively in his duties, the members at a meeting will meet and pass a vote of no confidence in him, and the position will be occupied by one of the elected ordinary members where applicable till the unexpired term is completed, or the position will be filled by an election.

GUIDELINES OF THE MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE

All members of the Management Committee shall hold office until the conclusion of the Annual General Meeting of the group next after their election but shall be eligible for reelection. A term in office shall run for a period of one year. The Management Committee shall convene meetings at least once in a month. The meeting may be conducted online or in-person and a notice for such meetings should be communicated at least five days to the date of the meeting.

The Management Committee has the power to co-opt ordinary committee members provided that the number of co-opted members shall not exceed two thirds of the total membership of the Management Committee. Co-opted members shall be entitled to vote at meetings of the Committee.

The Management Committee may appoint such special or standing committee or sub-committee as may be considered necessary and shall determine their terms of reference, powers, duration and composition. All acts and proceedings of such special or standing committees or sub-committees shall be reported back to the Management Committee as soon as possible.

Each member of the Management Committee, including the co-opted members, shall have one vote and in addition the Chairperson shall have a casting vote should this prove necessary.

Minutes shall be taken of all Management Committee and subcommittee meetings and shall contain a record of all proceedings, resolutions and decisions.

Only committee members with 18 years and above of age will be involved in legal arrangements.

Any member who ceases to be a member in the Pinnacle Investors Group ceases to be a bearer of the office.

Office bearers may be removed from office in the same way as is laid down for the expulsion of members and vacancies thus created shall be filled by persons elected at the annual general meeting resolving the expulsion.
