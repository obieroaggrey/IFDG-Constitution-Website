ARTICLE THREE – SECTION V

MEETINGS OF THE GROUP

The following procedures shall be observed at all meetings of the Association; There shall be six (6) general meetings of the Group each year, one at the beginning of every trading period and the sixth one (AGM) at the end of the year. All meetings of the Group shall be held at a conference room of choice by the project coordinator and on such date as may be determined by the Management Committee. At the instance of the Management Committee, a virtual emergency meeting of members shall be convened. The Secretary shall give notice and the agenda to be discussed two days prior to the meeting. The Secretary shall provide the agenda or details of the business to be transacted at all meetings of the Association, two days prior to the meeting. Two thirds (2/3) of the total membership of the Group shall form a quorum at all meetings including an emergency meeting. Any member of staff desirous of submitting, for consideration, any sort of resolution would be required to give one (1) weeks’ notice to the Secretary before the date of the meeting. Notwithstanding the above, the Management Committee may admit resolutions submitted for consideration outside the time frame given. Any resolution passed by a majority of the staff present at any meeting of the Group after due notice has been given to all the members of staff shall be effective for all purposes.

ANNUAL GENERAL MEETINGS

The Annual General Meeting of the Group shall be held at such time and at such place as the Management Committee shall determine. At least 21 clear days' notice of the meeting shall be given in writing by the secretary to each member. At such Annual General Meeting the business shall include:

(a) The consideration of the annual report of the Management Committee,

(b) The approval of the audited accounts, the election of the Management Committee

(c) The appointment of the auditor or auditors,

(d) Motions submitted by the Management Committee, or by members and the transaction of such matters as may from time to time be necessary.

2) The Chairperson of the Management Committee may at any time at his/her discretion call a Special General Meeting of the Group, the chair has the power to call for a consultative meeting at the end of every month and a general meeting at the end of every financial year which shall run for a period of six months. The secretary shall give a notice of the Special General Meeting of the group within 21 days of receiving a written request so to do signed by not less than TWO THIRDS of partners whether individual or representative and giving reasons for the request. At least 21 clear days' notice of the meeting shall be given in writing or SMS by the secretary to each member.

3) All the special general meetings shall however be online through the zoom or google meet at a time that will be agreeable by at least two thirds of the members. Two thirds of all the members shall constitute a quorum in all official meetings discussed in this section

All resolutions of the Management committee are subject to consideration by the MEMBERS
