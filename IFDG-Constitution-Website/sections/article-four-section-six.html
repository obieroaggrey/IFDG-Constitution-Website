ARTICLE FOUR

SECTION VI

Dissolution

If the Management Committee decides at any time that on the ground of expense or otherwise it is necessary or advisable to dissolve the Group, it shall call a Special General Meeting of the Group in accordance with Article VI (2) stating the terms of the resolution to be proposed. If the decision shall be confirmed by two-thirds majority of those present and entitled to vote the Management Committee shall have the power to dispose off any assets held by or on behalf of the Group.

Any assets remaining after the satisfaction of any proper debts and liabilities shall be given or transferred to such other charitable organizations having objects similar to the objects of the Group, as the Committee may determine.
