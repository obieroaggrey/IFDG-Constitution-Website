ARTICLE TWO

SECTION I: PURPOSE OF ESTABLISHMENT

Purpose of the establishment:

Mission Statement:

To use a practical approach to help the members realize their full potential, increase incomes and reduce overdependence on the sources of income, through partnership with individuals, organizations and other stakeholders who share our vision.

THE GENERAL GOALS:

a) To develop partnership with private and public organizations

b) To develop programs to improve the living standards of her members.

cc) To facilitate financial and social growth of the members of this welfare group.

d) To educate and train members of the group on technical skills, financial literacy and entrepreneurship

e) To provide forums for sharing experiences and best practices f) To link members to groups or organizations with similar interests
