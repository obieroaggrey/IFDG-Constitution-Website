ARTICLE FOUR

SECTION I

FINANCES OF THE GROUP

The Welfare Association shall be funded via: a) Appeal for funds and group investments. b) Fines imposed on members. c) Seek donation or support from any relevant source.

Bankers: The monies of the Group shall be placed in such Bank Accounts as the Management Committee may determine and shall operate all the banking and any other accounts.

Signatories: The following officers shall at all times act together as signatories to the account:

i) The Chairman

ii) General Secretary

iii) The Treasurer

All monies raised on behalf of the Group shall be applied to further the objectives of the GROUP and for no other purpose.

No office bearer shall be paid or given a sum of money or honorarium except in so far as it is as a payment to reimburse that member for outlays actually incurred on the business of the Group and for its benefit

The accounting period of the Group shall run for four months.

The treasurer shall keep proper accounts of the finances of the Group.

The accounts shall be audited at least once in an accounting period by the auditor or the auditors appointed at the Annual General Meeting.

An audited statement of the accounts for the previous fiscal year shall be submitted by the Management Committee to the Annual General Meeting.
