Preamble

On a mutual call and mutual concern to uplift our economic power and create wealth for ourselves and our people, this group adopted this constitution as PINNACLE INVESTORS forward on how to use the available business and investment opportunities amongst our people. To change the economic narrative and to inspire hope amongst our people, to make them believe that we can as well thrive in business as we do in academics and politics. We the members of the abo GROUP, do hereby adopt and give to ourselves this Constitution for the management of the Group.
