ARTICLE FOUR

SECTION III

Alterations to the Constitution

Any amendment to any article or section in this constitution shall be made upon confirmation of receipt of such amendment by the secretary to the members present in the meeting from a member pursuing the amendment in writing days before the official meeting.

Any alterations to this constitution shall receive the assent of not less than two thirds of the members of the Group present and voting at a General Meeting whether Annual or Special [in accordance with Article VI (2)].

Notice of such a meeting, setting forth the terms of the proposed alteration(s), must be sent by the secretary to each member of the Group. No alteration shall be made which would the effect of causing the Group to cease to be recognized as a FINANCIAL DEVELOPMENT GROUP.

No amendments to the constitution shall be implemented without a prior consent in writing of the registrar, obtained upon application to him/her made in writing and signed by three of the office bearers.
