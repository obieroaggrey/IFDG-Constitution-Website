ARTICLE THREE

SECTION VII

Rules of All Meetings

All phones to be on silent mode while the meeting is in progress. No use of mobile phones in whichever means other than the one agreed upon (if any) at the meeting. Any member who violates this act shall be fined 20/- instantly through the office of the chair or the person presiding over the meeting as at that time. A member who arrives at the meeting 10 minutes late to pay a fine of 10/- and those arriving one hour after to pay a fine Of 20/=

A member who leaves the meeting prematurely without informing the chair regardless of time of living will be subjected to a fine of 20/=

Absence from the physical meeting without a valid apology to attract a fine of 500/=. All apologies for absence from such meetings to be given through the chairperson but with a fine of Ksh. 100/=.

Absence from the online meetings without a valid apology to attract a fine of 50/=. All apologies to be given through the chairperson but with a fine of 20/-

All the fines charged on members at the meeting to be paid through the official Mpesa number 0758616058 not later than one week from the date of the meeting otherwise it attracts a penalty of 50% of the fine and it shall be recovered from the shares
