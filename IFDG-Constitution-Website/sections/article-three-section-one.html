ARTICLE THREE – SECTION I

SAVINGS, CREDIT AND INTEREST CHARGED

a) Members to make a weekly subscription of amount not less than 100/ - on or before the Sunday of the following week which will directly go to the individual’s shares and must be deposited directly to the group KCB Account number

b) members to also make a monthly subscription of 30/= for the BBF kitty

c)Members to make also monthly subscription of 50/- for development

NOTE: Monies sent after Sunday midnight to attract a penalty of 10/-.Monies sent more than one week after that Sunday at every week to attract a fine of 20/-

d)In the event of a financial need, a member shall be allowed to borrow up to 60% of his personal shares in the kitty and the amount borrowed attracts an interest of 8% per month.

e) However, upon being issued a loan the group shall deduct a service charge of 4% on the amount borrowed to cover the transaction cost and group’s assets but upon repaying the loan one must always pay the full amount borrowed plus the interest.

e) 50% of interest earned from an amount not exceeding 60% of the borrower’s share shall go directly to his dividends and the other 50% is shared amongst the members equally.

f) All loans below 5000/- to members shall run for a period of at most three months while loans of 5,000/- and above shall run for at most five months with interest of 8% per month charged on amount due respectively, however at the lapse of the third month and fifth month respectively the amount due shall be recovered from the loanee’s shares.

g) A member can however get a loan of any amount exceeding 60% of the member’s shares in the group but upon being guaranteed by a member (guarantor) whose shares can meet the borrower’s financial need as at the time. These monies shall attract an interest of 8% per month on amount due. Such interests shall be shared as follows: 25% goes directly to the guarantor’s shares,25% goes directly to the borrower’s shares while 50% is to be shared to all members equally.

h) All loan requests shall be made in writing (soft copy form/hard copy) to the chairperson of Impulsive Investors Self Help Group who shall approve or reject the loan request and make a communication to the treasurer for proper action in case of approval.

i) The group may also give financial assistance in the form of loans to non-members. The loan must be guaranteed by a member of the group before it is disbursed. The loan shall run for a period of one month on the maximum at an interest of per day

j) Interest earned will be directed to the members’ respective shares which shall be disbursed at the end of every financial year in the form of dividends on a date that shall be decided on by not less than two thirds of the members.

k) The group however, gives a provision of buying and selling of shares for any member willing to venture into such as follows;

i) A share shall be costed at 100/-. All members are therefore supposed to have a whole number share-there shall not be a fraction of a share.

i) A share is bought by the group at KSH. 80 and sold at KSH. 90 such that for every share bought and sold the group gets a profit of KSH. 10

ii) A member shouldn't be allowed to buy/sell shares directly without passing through the group.

L} For every given financial year, every member is encouraged to borrow a loan from the group at least once.

M }In case a member withdraws from the group before the end of a financial period, they forfeit the interest accrued on the dividends (A financial period begins 1st January to 31st January of the following year)
