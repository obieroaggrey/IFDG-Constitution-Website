ARTICLE THREE – SECTION II

MEMBERSHIP

1. Membership and Subscription

A) Membership of the Group shall be open to: -

a) Individuals who are interested in furthering the aims of the Group;

b) Organizations having similar objectives.

c) Persons willing to pay registration fee as at the time of joining (non-refundable upon withdrawal of the member from the group).

d) At the inception of the group, the pioneer members all contributed a registration fee of KSH 150/-

(2) The Management Committee shall have powers to establish a membership subscription and to make recommendations to a General Meeting on the level of such a subscription.

(3) The Management Committee shall have the right to terminate the membership of any individual or organization appearing to have brought the Group into disrepute or to have acted in a fraudulent manner, provided that the individual member concerned or the individual representing such organization (as the case may be) shall have the right to be heard by the Management Committee before a final decision is made. Any member expelled from the Group shall have the right to appeal within one month of such expulsion. The Committee may, however, at its discretion, reinstate such a member. Any member desiring to resign from the group shall submit his or her resignation to the secretary, which shall take effect from the date of receipt by the secretary of such notice upon confirmation by the chair i.e., should give one-month notice or pay for damages if the notice given is less than one month. The group shall have membership not exceeding 18 Any person or organization who resigns or is removed from membership shall be entitled to a refund of 100% of his or her total shares.

MEMBERSHIP

Any person of or over the age of eighteen (18) years who shares the above interests with reliable source of income and shows commitment Shall be eligible for membership of the Group and shall, subject to approval of the management committee, become a member on payment of the prescribed membership fee (KSH 250) which is nonrefundable. The Group attracts a minimum of eleven (11) members as per now and a maximum of eighteen (18) members.

CESSATION OF MEMBERSHIP/EXISTS/SEPARATIONS

When one is no longer interested in being a member of the Group, he/she may resign from the organization by writing to the Executive Council for approval.

Any member upon death

A member found guilty of gross misconduct such as false information to obtain benefits from the group, corruption, use of abusive language against another member shall cease to be a member of the Association upon review and determination by the discipline committee.

A member who exits the Group shall be entitled to a refund 95% of his or her total contribution.

Any member shall be expelled from membership if the committee so recommends and if a General meeting of the Group shall resolve by two thirds majority that such a member should be expelled on the grounds that his/her conduct has adversely affected the reputation and dignity of the Group

Nominees

Every member shall nominate a nominee in writing as his/ her nominee to whom, upon their death, deposits shall be vested and such appointments shall be attested by two witnesses who are members of the group. In the event of death of a member, the group after satisfying itself and after obtaining such documentary proof of the death of a member as it may consider necessary, shall pay to the nominee the value of the deceased member’s deposits, after deducting such sums as may be due by the member to the group, as soon as possible.

Burial Benevolent Fund

Article 3 Section 1, paragraph (b) roman (iii) articulates the BBF fund's contributions.

This is money collected and a percentage as in sub article (c) is given to a member who qualifies for the withdrawal of the BBF fund from the account.

a) Attributes that qualifies withdrawal of a fraction of BBF funds

i) death of a member and or his/her close relatives limited to his/her children,spouse,siblings, mother and the father.

ii) Graduation of the primary member.

b) Percentage contributions and division of the BBF fund to those who qualify.

i) Apart from death, members who qualify for the other attributes of benefiting from BBF kitty will get a 10% of the members’ contribution for the same attribute. This money will be from the BBF account.

ii) In case of death:

I) of the primary member, each member will contribute 200/= for bereavement and 5000/=from BBF account and the money handed to a family member that was nominated by the member. (Incase the BFF kitty cannot raise the cash, we borrow from the Groups account)

II) of a spouse, child/children, each member will contribute 150/= for bereavement and a3500/= from BBF account and the money handed to the member. (Incase the BFF kitty cannot raise the cash, we borrow from the Groups account)

III) of a parent, each member will contribute 150/= and3500 from BBF account and the money handed to the member. (Incase the BFF kitty cannot raise the cash, we borrow from the Groups account)

IV) of a sibling each member will contribute 100/= and 2500 from the BBF account and the money handed to the member. (Incase the BBF kitty cannot raise the cash, we borrow from the Group’s account).
